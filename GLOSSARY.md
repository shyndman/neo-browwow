# Domain Language

**Document Type**:
The editorial form of a document — such as a research paper, blog post, encyclopedia entry, or news article — that governs how its sections and inline elements are spoken aloud.
_Avoid_: Document category, document kind, genre

**Sentence**:
A contiguous inline span within a paragraph that forms a natural unit of speech.
_Avoid_: Utterance, segment, chunk

**Parenthetical**:
A round-parenthesized `(...)` or square-bracketed `[...]` inline span within a paragraph that can be classified and conditionally omitted from speech generation.
_Avoid_: Aside, bracketed span

**Text Span**:
An inline range within a paragraph — such as a sentence or parenthetical — defined by its start and end text leaves and their lowest common ancestor.
_Avoid_: Wrapper node, span container
